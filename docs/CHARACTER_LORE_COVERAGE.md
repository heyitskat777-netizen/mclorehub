Magic/CA/FA is a sheet selection kind. Branches or mixed-source practices are not
extra independent slots merely because they share a source. CArts/MArts, hubs,
inventions and disconnection notices are not automatically offered. Herblore is
recorded as a feat; Shapeshifting trial redlines explicitly require an FA, although
its stored tier section retains older MA wording. No application is verified.

Rules cover only the specific pairings in [Phase 9](PHASE_9.md). Seer/Templar and
Seer/Cleric remain source disagreements. Sprite/Herblore and Seer/Shapeshifting
produce conditional requirements notices. Zero warnings does not mean compatible.

Each affected source update suspends its curated choices/checks until curation
matches the new revision; it never deletes a character entry. See the
[Phase 8 curation procedure](PHASE_8.md#future-curation-procedure) for adding sources.
